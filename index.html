Our story

The Tree, The Heart, The Roots

The Tree of Life is one of humanity's oldest symbols. It speaks a language that needs no translation: what grows above is sustained by what lives below.

A child's ability to reach, to learn, to connect, to flourish, is only as strong as the roots they grow from. Emotional regulation, breathwork, nervous system resilience. These are the roots. They are invisible to most of the world, but they hold everything up.

At the center of our logo, where trunk meets earth, there is a heart. Not at the top. Not in the crown. Love is not the reward for growth. It is the origin of it.

We don't start at the leaves.
We start at the roots.
And we begin with love.

With roots and resolve,
Adam Jacobs
Founder, Little Roots Foundation
